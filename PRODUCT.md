# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People in African savings and credit groups (AVEC), group officers, and platform administrators. Public visitors need to understand the service and create a member account before joining or creating a group.

## Product Purpose

AVEC Microcredit organizes group savings, community credit, member communication, and public member content in one web and PWA platform.

## Positioning

The platform keeps personal member accounts separate from group roles and balances while making group governance, contributions, and member communication available in the same service.

## Operating Context

Members access the service primarily on mobile web. Financial operations, phone verification, wallet top-ups, and Momo integrations are currently SANDBOX workflows; no real operator payment is claimed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The application provides identity and PIN authentication, groups, savings and credit workflows, internal wallet transfers, social and group chat, moderation, and administration. Real SMS, Momo/card payments, KYC/AML, and WebRTC calling remain external integrations. The public entry point must lead people to the existing member registration and connection flows without duplicating financial controls.

## Brand Commitments

The product is named AVEC Microcredit. Its French copy must be direct, clear, and honest about SANDBOX limits.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing application routes and interfaces in `public/` demonstrate group creation, member registration, news, wallet, and administration. No external customer testimonials, performance figures, real payment-provider claims, or brand imagery are available.

## Product Principles

- Make group participation understandable before asking for account details.
- Keep real-world financial claims verifiable and clearly separate from SANDBOX demonstrations.
- Preserve clear, accessible actions on mobile devices.
- Keep public discovery separate from authenticated financial operations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Controls require visible focus, accessible names, and readable French labels. Icon-only navigation retains programmatic labels and explanatory tooltips.
